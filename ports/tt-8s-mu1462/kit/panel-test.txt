# Our own cockpit drawing, first test (tt-zoom v19): coloured boxes with a label, 10 s each.
# Note which colours appear and where; that maps out the coordinate system.
say panel test start
panel info
wait 2000
say P1 RED box at 0,0 (front layer)
panel show front 0 0 300 120 c00000 P1 RED 0,0
wait 10000
say P2 GREEN box at 400,150 (front layer)
panel show front 400 150 300 120 00a000 P2 GREEN 400,150
wait 10000
say P3 BLUE box at 900,300 (front layer)
panel show front 900 300 300 120 0040c0 P3 BLUE 900,300
wait 10000
say P4 MAGENTA box at -150,-60 (front layer, in case 0,0 is the centre)
panel show front -150 -60 300 120 b000b0 P4 MAGENTA centre
wait 10000
say P5 ORANGE box at 400,150 (screens layer)
panel show screens 400 150 300 120 d07000 P5 ORANGE screens
wait 10000
say panel hidden
panel hide
wait 4000
loop
